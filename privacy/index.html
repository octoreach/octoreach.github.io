English

1. Introduction

One Flow ("we", "our", or "us") is a focus timer and deep-work tracking app. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app handles, how it is used, and how you can control it.

2. Focus Data

Your focus sessions, timer settings, session notes, topics, and timeline records are stored on your device by default. We do not sell your focus data.

3. Analytics and Diagnostics

One Flow uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand app usage, diagnose crashes, and improve reliability. These services may collect device information, app interactions, crash logs, diagnostic data, and approximate usage events. This information is processed by Google Firebase according to its privacy and security practices.

4. Cloud Sync and Purchases

If cloud sync or premium purchases are enabled in a future release, the app may use account, purchase, and synchronization services to provide those features. Any such data use must be reflected in the store privacy disclosures for the release that enables it.

5. Data Control and Deletion

You can delete local focus data by deleting sessions in the app when supported, clearing app data, or uninstalling the app. You may contact us to ask privacy questions or request assistance.

中文

1. 简介

One Flow（壹心流，“我们”）是一款专注计时和深度工作记录应用。本隐私政策说明应用会处理哪些数据、这些数据如何使用，以及您如何控制这些数据。

2. 专注数据

您的专注会话、计时设置、会话笔记、主题和时间线记录默认存储在您的设备本地。我们不会出售您的专注数据。

3. 分析与诊断

One Flow 使用 Firebase Analytics 和 Firebase Crashlytics 来了解应用使用情况、诊断崩溃并提升稳定性。这些服务可能收集设备信息、应用交互、崩溃日志、诊断数据和大致使用事件。相关信息会由 Google Firebase 按其隐私和安全实践处理。

4. 云同步与购买

如果未来版本启用云同步或高级版购买，应用可能会使用账号、购买和同步服务来提供对应功能。启用这些功能的版本必须在商店隐私披露中同步说明对应数据使用方式。

5. 数据控制与删除

您可以在应用支持时删除本地会话记录，也可以通过清除应用数据或卸载应用删除本地专注数据。您也可以联系我们咨询隐私问题或请求协助。
