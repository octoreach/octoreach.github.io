FAQ

How do I start a focus session?

Choose a focus mode on the home screen, adjust the timer if needed, then tap START.

What happens when a timer finishes?

When a focus round finishes, One Flow shows a settlement state where you can rest briefly, start the next focus round, or finish and review the session.

How do breaks work?

After a focus round, you can start a short rest. Longer breaks can be introduced after repeated focus rounds depending on the timer flow.

Where can I review my focus history?

Use the Timeline tab to review recent sessions and the Statistics tab to understand focus patterns and progress.

Data and deletion

Is my data synced?

Current focus records are stored locally unless a cloud sync feature is explicitly enabled in the app.

What data leaves my device?

Focus sessions, timer settings, notes, topics, and timeline records stay on your device by default. One Flow uses Firebase diagnostics to understand app usage and crashes, so limited usage and diagnostic signals may be processed by Firebase. Accounts, cloud sync, and purchases are not active in this release.

How can I remove my data?

You can clear local focus data by clearing app data in system settings or uninstalling the app. If a future release enables cloud sync or purchases, the privacy policy and store disclosures must be updated for that release.
